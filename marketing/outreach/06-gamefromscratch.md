## During vetting — message

~~~~text
Hi GameFromScratch,

I'm Houtamelo, a C# and Rust developer. I've submitted Spire, a Unity C# package, for Asset Store vetting.

Spire catches missing switch cases at compile time and offers IDE quick fixes to generate the branches. It supports annotated enums and class/interface hierarchies, as well as generated discriminated unions whose variants carry different typed payloads.

The Unity integration includes generated Inspector drawers and JSON converters. Runtime unions can also be used in Burst/DOTS when their payloads meet Burst's restrictions.

I thought this could fit your coverage of game-development tools: the concrete workflow is to add a new gameplay command, follow the missing-case diagnostics to affected switches, and generate the branches in the IDE.

The package minimum is Unity 2022.3.62f3. Rider and Visual Studio Community workflows have been verified. The regular Asset Store price will be US$20; approval and publication are pending.
~~~~
